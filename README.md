Create a charming, whimsical LOW-POLY FOUNTAIN for IDLE EXPRESS, a cozy toy train simulator set in a colorful miniature countryside world.

IMPORTANT STYLE MATCH:
The game world has soft green grass, geometric low-poly pine trees, pastel pink flowering trees, small colorful houses, simple railway tracks, and a peaceful toy-town atmosphere. The fountain must look like it belongs naturally in this exact environment.

DESIGN:
A delightful circular town-square fountain made from warm cream-colored stone, muted pastel teal accents, soft sage green details, and subtle warm golden trim. Use chunky rounded geometric shapes, clean low-poly surfaces, slightly irregular handcrafted details, and a friendly storybook aesthetic.

Create a medium-sized two-tier fountain with a broad shallow circular basin, a smaller upper bowl, a simple sculpted centerpiece, and several elegant curved water spouts. Add a few stylized water streams flowing into the basin. The design should feel playful, peaceful, and magical without looking luxurious or futuristic.

VISUAL DETAILS:
- Soft mint and turquoise water
- Cream stone with subtle warm beige variation
- Small pastel teal decorative tiles
- Simple leaf and flower motifs
- A few small sculpted flowers around the outer rim
- Chunky stone supports with clean geometric shapes
- Gentle, inviting proportions suitable for a miniature train town

ART DIRECTION:
Stylized low-poly 3D game asset, cozy countryside diorama, handcrafted toy aesthetic, pastel color palette, simple readable silhouette, charming rather than realistic, consistent with Roblox low-poly scenery.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- One standalone fountain model
- No surrounding plaza or ground
- No trees, houses, trains, benches, or other separate scenery
- No text, logos, or watermark
- Clean geometry and sensible centered origin
- Upright orientation
- Moderate polygon count, optimized for Roblox real-time gameplay
- Keep the basin and decorative elements structurally simple
- Water geometry should be simple; animated water effects can be added later in Roblox Studio

The final fountain should look like the heart of a peaceful miniature countryside train station: beautiful, welcoming, colorful, and perfectly integrated into the existing low-poly toy world.

Avoid photorealism, excessive gold, marble palace architecture, intricate baroque ornaments, metallic luxury finishes, futuristic design, and overly complex geometry.
